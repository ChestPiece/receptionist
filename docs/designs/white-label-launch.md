# Design: Selling Sara as a white-label product to Dubai clinics

## Context
Sara works end to end on synthetic clinics: WhatsApp, web chat, voice, a front-desk CRM, and per-clinic branding and persona. Anas is unsure how to turn it into something clinics pay for. Facts from this session:
- No clinic has seen it yet (pre-product).
- Anas lives in Pakistan. His brother lives in the UAE and has a company phone number.
- Anas will do all outreach from that number. His brother only meets interested clinics in person.
- Anas wants the product to support the real, paid production setup that clinics will use. While developing, he keeps testing on free tiers.

## Problem statement
A Dubai clinic's after-hours WhatsApp messages go unanswered, and some are lost for good; staff never get back to them. Each lost message is a lost visit worth about AED 300 to 1,500. Sara answers and books at night. The question is how to package, price, and ship this so a Dubai clinic pays, sold remotely from Pakistan.

## Demand evidence
None yet. "Unanswered, sometimes lost" is Anas's account of the status quo. It has not yet been confirmed by a clinic. This is the biggest risk, and the plan is ordered around it.

## Premises (agreed)
1. Sell directly to Dubai clinics first. Anas does outreach from his brother's UAE company WhatsApp number, and his brother handles in-person meetings. Agencies (true white-label resale) come only after 2 or 3 clinics are live with numbers.
2. The wedge is "no lost after-hours WhatsApp", proven by a weekly report: "Sara booked N visits worth AED X while you were closed." The CRM is a bonus, not what is being sold.
3. Onboard the first 3 clinics by hand with a script. Build self-serve onboarding only after that.
4. Real patient data must be hosted inside the UAE. Federal Law 2/2019 (ICT Health Law), Art. 13, sets fines of AED 500k to 700k. Today's Supabase and Vercel regions are not in the UAE, so the move happens before clinic #1 goes live.
5. A paying clinic gets the official Meta WhatsApp Cloud API. UltraMsg stays for demos and testing only, because of the ban risk.

## Approaches considered
- **A. Concierge launch.** Sales kit, scripted manual onboarding, weekly owner report. *Chosen*, with the paid-stack switches below.
- **B. Full platform first** (admin, self-serve, billing, UAE hosting, before any outreach). Rejected: it means weeks of build with no buyer.
- **C. Agency SaaS mode** (custom domains, agency branding). Rejected for now: agencies want proof first. Revisit after 3 to 5 live clinics.

## Recommended approach: A, built so the paid production stack is a per-clinic switch
The principle: every paid production choice is config, not a rewrite. Anas tests each one for free, so a signed clinic only needs credentials pasted in.

### 1. Meta WhatsApp Cloud API as a second provider (build now)
- `src/lib/whatsapp.ts` already isolates the provider. Add a `meta` provider next to `ultramsg`:
  - send text with `POST graph.facebook.com/<ver>/<phone_number_id>/messages`
  - mark as read
  - parse the inbound webhook
- A new migration adds `clinics.wa_provider` (`'ultramsg' | 'meta'`, default `ultramsg`) and `clinics.meta_phone_number_id`. It also adds `clinic_secrets.meta_token` and `meta_app_secret`, which stay service-role only, as today.
- New route `/api/whatsapp/meta`:
  - `GET` answers the `hub.challenge` verify request.
  - `POST` checks the `X-Hub-Signature-256` HMAC. This is better than today's secret in the query string.
  - The payload's `phone_number_id` selects the clinic, the way `instanceId` does now.
  - It then calls the same `handleMessage`.
- **Templates:** Meta allows free-form messages only within 24 h of the patient's last message. Reminders and follow-ups in `src/lib/jobs.ts` must therefore send approved template messages (EN and AR) when the provider is `meta`.
- **Test free:** a Meta developer app gives a free test number that can message up to 5 verified recipient numbers. Use Anas's and his brother's phones.

### 2. Portable hosting for the UAE move (prepare now, move at clinic #1)
- Keep the app runnable outside Vercel. Use Next `output: "standalone"` plus a Dockerfile, and check that `after()` and cron work on a plain Node host. The cron is pg_cron calling a URL, so it stays the same.
- Target at clinic #1: a UAE-region VM (Azure UAE North or AWS me-central-1) running the app and self-hosted Supabase (official docker-compose). The other option is managed Postgres in-region, with Supabase Auth self-hosted.
- **Test free:** run the docker-compose stack locally, apply migrations 0001 to 0012 plus the seed, and run the Good Shine flows against it.
- **Open legal point:** patient text also goes to OpenAI, which is outside the UAE. Options are Azure OpenAI in UAE North (the Agents SDK can point at it) or a legal opinion. Decide this together with the hosting move.

### 3. Proof engine: weekly owner report (build now)
- Each Sunday the cron sends the clinic owner a WhatsApp message (`clinics.owner_whatsapp`, or `staff_whatsapp` as a fallback). It covers the past week:
  - visits booked by Sara
  - how many of those were booked after hours
  - estimated AED, from `services.price_aed`
  - chats handed to staff
  - no-shows prevented by reminder confirms
- It reads the same data as the Today tallies (`src/app/dashboard/page.tsx`). Put the queries in one shared helper.
- Show the same numbers on the Today page as a "This week, Sara earned" card. This is what the brother shows in the meeting.

### 4. Concierge onboarding script (build now)
- Turn `scripts/seed-good-shine.ts` into `scripts/new-clinic.ts`. It takes a JSON file with:
  - name, slug, hours, services and prices, dentists
  - branding and persona
  - WhatsApp provider plus credentials
  - owner email
- It creates the clinic, the secrets row, the owner membership, and an invite link. It is idempotent, and it never writes secrets to tracked files.
- Target: a new clinic is live in under 1 hour.

### 5. Billing: no code yet
- Invoice in AED through the brother's UAE company. First confirm that its trade-licence activity covers IT and software services.
- If it does not, use a merchant of record (Paddle or Lemon Squeezy) or a UAE free-zone licence later. Stripe does not onboard Pakistan entities.
- Track plan and usage per clinic from the existing `usage_events` table. Usage caps come at clinic #5.

### 6. How a clinic connects its own WhatsApp
The clinic picks one of three paths in onboarding. Store the choice in `clinics.wa_provider`.
1. **Keep the clinic's current number (recommended): Meta "coexistence".**
   - The clinic's existing WhatsApp Business app number is joined to the Cloud API through Meta Embedded Signup (`whatsapp_business_app_onboarding`).
   - Staff keep using the phone app, and Sara answers through the API on the same number.
   - Messages staff send from the phone arrive as echo webhooks. Treat a staff echo like a desk reply: pause Sara for that chat (reuse `stillPaused` and `setPaused`).
   - Caveats: app version 2.24.17 or later, a sync that can take up to 24 h, and linked companion devices get unlinked once.
   - Build against the current Embedded Signup version (v2 was deprecated 2026-10-15).
2. **A new dedicated number on the Cloud API.** Simplest technically. The clinic advertises a new "book on WhatsApp" number.
3. **UltraMsg QR link.** Demo and testing only, never a paying clinic.

Website: the chat widget embed (`widget.js` plus the `allowed_origins` check, already planned) lets a clinic add Sara to its own website.

Phone calls: today "Call Sara" is a browser call. Real phone calls are a separate item to research, not part of the first sale:
- WhatsApp Business Calling API plus OpenAI Realtime SIP, or a SIP trunk.
- UAE VoIP rules (TDRA) limit which providers can terminate calls.

### 7. Running costs per clinic (pay per use, nothing burns while idle)
Assumed workload: 600 chats/month, 300 visits with 2 reminders each, 100 follow-ups, 150 web calls of 3 min.

| Item | Rate (vendor and blog figures, check Meta and OpenAI rate cards) | Est./month |
|---|---|---|
| Chat model (gpt-5-mini, cached prompt prefix, reviewer only on risky turns) | ~$0.02 to 0.04 per chat | $12 to 25 |
| Voice (gpt-realtime mini, ~$10/$20 per 1M audio tokens in/out) | ~$0.03 to 0.06 per min | $15 to 30 |
| WhatsApp replies in the 24 h service window | free (sources say free up to 1,000 per number per month; confirm) | ~$0 |
| Reminders (utility template, UAE ~$0.0157) | 600 × $0.0157 | ~$9 |
| Follow-ups (marketing template, UAE ~$0.0576 from 2026-10-01) | 100 × $0.0576 | ~$6 |
| UAE hosting share (one VM for several clinics) | ~$40 to 80 per VM | ~$10 to 20 |
| **Total** | | **~$50 to 90 ≈ AED 185 to 330** |

Rules so money is never spent without use:
- Go to Meta directly, without a BSP (Twilio or 360dialog markups and monthly fees).
- Keep `allow()` rate limits and add a monthly cap per tier from `usage_events`. Alert at 80%, and degrade to "staff will reply" at 100%; never cut a booking mid-chat.
- Keep the mini models and prompt caching as they are.
- Send follow-ups only to warm inquiries. That is already the rule; it keeps marketing-template spend small.

### Pricing (starting point, to be tested in meetings)
Position Sara as the receptionist that never misses an overflow message or a call at 22:00, not as a replacement for staff. Two receptionists cost about AED 6,000/month and still miss messages at peak times. Sara costs a fraction of one receptionist, and the CRM shows what she caught.
| Tier | AED/month | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | 799 | WhatsApp + web chat, reminders, CRM, 1 location, up to 3 dentists |
| Clinic | 1,499 | + voice calls, follow-ups, weekly report, up to 8 dentists |
| Group | custom | multi-location |

- Setup fee: AED 1,500, waived for the first 3 "founding" clinics, which also keep their price for 12 months.
- Meta message fees are included up to the tier cap; above the cap they are passed through at cost.
- Margin check: running cost is about AED 185 to 330 per clinic, against AED 799 to 1,499 in revenue. That is a gross margin of about 60 to 80%. Starter excludes voice to protect its margin.
- Market check: dental AI receptionists sell for about $299 to $1,500 per location per month. A human receptionist costs AED 4,000 to 7,000 per month.
